#### Event Description

The Quality Enhancement Cell of Peshawar Campus arranged a 1-Day training workshop for capacity building of the desk staff on Lean Office Principles for Leadership on Friday 17th September 2021. The initiative was taken on the basis of the results of the Staff Satisfaction Survey 2020. All staff members along with Final Year Project Coordinators also attended the workshop.
The event was facilitated by a training and management consultancy firm named Center for Awareness Training and Development (CATD) which was actively participated by all the participants.
The event ended with a closing ceremony where certificates were distributed among the participants and a shield as a token of appreciation was presented to the CATD from NUCES-FAST.
