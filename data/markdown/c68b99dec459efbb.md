#### Event Description

The NCEAC (National Computing Education Accreditation Council) team visited the Department of Computer Science, Peshawar Campus on Monday, 11th October, 2021 for the accreditation of BS (Computer Science) program, batch 17, 18 and 19. The team consisted of Dr. Naveed Ikram (Convener) - Professor, Faculty of Computing, Riphah International University, Islamabad and Dr. Muhammad Muzammil (Member) – Associate Professor, Bahria University, Islamabad. During the visit, the NCEAC team was briefed by the Dean Computing, Director Campus and the Head of Computer Science Department about the salient features of the various computing programs offered by the Peshawar campus followed by a detailed presentation by Assistant Professor Dr. Taimoor Khan. The NCEAC team also visited various campus facilities and expressed their satisfaction on the overall program execution as well as physical infrastructure. The visit was successfully concluded with an exit meeting with the Director campus and the Head of Computer Science Department.
