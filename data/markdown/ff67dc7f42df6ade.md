## Department of Science and Humanities

### Dr. Ihsan Ullah

##### Assistant Professor

##### **(HEC Approved)**

##### [ihsan.ullah@nu.edu.pk](mailto:ihsan.ullah@nu.edu.pk)

Dr. Ihsan Ullah has been serving as an Assistant Professor of Finance since 2013 at FAST-NUCES, Peshawar Campus. Before that, he served in the same organization as a lecturer for four years. He also worked as a Financial Services Administrator at Clydesdale Bank, Glasgow. Dr. Ihsan completed his MBA (Finance) from the University of Peshawar (2003) and his M.Sc. Money, Banking, and Finance from Middlesex University, London (2007). He achieved distinction in Economic Theory, Corporate Finance, and Investment. He also accomplished PhD (Finance) from the Institute of Management Sciences in 2019. His teaching interests mainly include Financial Management, Corporate Finance, Investment, Business Statistics, and Microeconomics. Dr. Ihsan has published in Business & Economic Review, and Abasyn Journal of Social Sciences. He looks forward to a collaboration in research to publish on applications of machine learning in finance.
