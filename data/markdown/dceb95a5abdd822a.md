#### Event Description

Quality Enhancement Cell arranged an Assessment Team (AT) visit at Peshawar Campus for the purpose of evaluating the BS Software Engineering Program on November 10, 2022. The members of AT were Dr. Sajid Anwar, Associate Professor, Center for Excellence in IT from IM|Sciences Peshawar, and Dr. Musadaq Mansoor, Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science, NUCES, Peshawar. The AT evaluated the Self-assessment Report interacted with faculty members and students and went to departmental facilities as part of the visit to evaluate the program. In the Exit Meeting with higher management, the AT findings and report were shared at the visit's conclusion.
