#### Event Description

Quality Enhancement Cell arranged an Assessment Team (AT) visit at Peshawar Campus for the purpose of evaluating MS Data Science on December 20, 2022. The members of AT were Dr. Aamir Saeed, Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science & Information Technology, University of Engineering Technology, Jalozai, and Dr. Muhammad Amin, Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science, NUCES, Peshawar. The AT evaluated the Self-Assessment Report, interacted with faculty members and students, and went to departmental facilities as part of the visit to evaluate the program. In the Exit Meeting with higher management, the AT findings and report were shared at the visit's conclusion. The visit was arranged by the QEC-PWR.
