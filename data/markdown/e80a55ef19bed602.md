FAST – National University of Computer & Emerging Sciences, 160 Industrial Estate, Jamrud Road, Peshawar  Hostel Allocation Policy  * Preference is given to students who selected Peshawar Campus as their first campus preference during admission. * Hostel seats are allotted on a first-come, first-served basis. * Separate quotas are maintained for new and senior (returning) students. * Allocation is subject to seat availability and degree/batch-wise quotas. * Only complete applications will be processed. * Application timestamp is based on the date and time the email is received. * No separate confirmation email will be sent.
