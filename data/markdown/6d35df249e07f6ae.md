Hostel Rules & Regulations — for Residents 
FAST-NUCES Peshawar Campus 
Timings & Entry 
• Day scholars: no entry After 07:00 pm without permission + reception log. 
• Day scholars must leave hostel by 07:00 pm. 
• Boarders: back by 11:00 PM late arrival needs prior permission. 
• Outside food must be collected from main gate but not allowed after 11:00 pm. 
Rooms & Property 
• No room change/transfer without Warden's permission. 
• You are responsible for allotted items; return in good condition. 
• Damage costs are charged to the person responsible, once identified. 
• Report damage/repairs in the register at reception. 
• Furniture/mess property may not be moved out of place. 
• Rooms may be inspected for safety and must be done in the presence of students. 
Fees & Stay 
• Hostel terms: Fall (5 month), Spring (5 month), Summer (2 month), or Full Year (12 month). 
• Refunds follow the university's official Hostel Fee Refund Policy. 
• Clearance required at the end of each semester. 
• Hostel seat requires active/current registration status. 
Safety & Prohibited Items 
• Only low-power devices are allowed: laptop, phone charger, shaver. 
• Banned: heaters, fridges, kettles, irons, ACs, immersion rods (fire risk). 
• Coolers, fridges, irons, ACs, allowed only with written permission + extra charges. 
• No intoxicants (tobacco, velo, naswar, vape etc.). 
• No loud music, parties, or disturbance-causing activity. 
Conduct 
• Respect all hostel staff — zero tolerance for misbehavior. 
• Ragging/fooling of any kind is strictly banned (HEC Anti-Ragging Policy). 
• No guests; a paid guest room may be booked if available. 
• Secure your own valuables — hostel is not liable, but report incidents. 
• Responsibility for any incident is decided individually, based on evidence — not just presence. 
Discipline — Your Rights 
• You'll get a written notice before any penalty.  
• Cases are decided by the University Disciplinary Committee.  
Harassment & Grievances 
• Harassment complaints go to the Warden or University discipline committee /Anti-Harassment Committee — kept 
confidential. 
• No discrimination on religion, caste, gender, or region. 
• No victimization for a good-faith complaint. 
By signing this form, the student agrees to abide by these rules and the detailed Hostel Rules & Regulations document.
