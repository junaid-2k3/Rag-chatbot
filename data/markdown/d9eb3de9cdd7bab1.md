#### CM Undergraduate Scholarship Program (Extension in Dates)

* Posted On:  Oct 03, 24

The Punjab Chief Minister Honhaar Scholarship Program is now available for newly enrolled students in their 1st semester. This scholarship presents a significant opportunity for deserving students to support their academic journey.
To apply, visit [honhaarscholarship.punjabhec.gov.pk](https://honhaarscholarship.punjabhec.gov.pk) by 18 October 2024
