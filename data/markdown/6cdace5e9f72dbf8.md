#### Event Description

The NUCES QEC of Peshawar Campus collaborated with HR department of NUCES Islamabad Campus and sent non-faculty staff from Peshawar Campus to attend a 2-Day workshop on “Toolkit for Effective Professionals”. Workshop was held on 25-26 November, 2019 at NUCES Islamabad Campus. Mr. Ghulam Ali Abid, Manager-MIS, Mr. Miraj Ahmad, Deputy Manager-MIS, Mr. Hamid Mansoor, Deputy Manager-Library and Mr. Midrar ul Haq, Office Assitant attended the mentioned workshop.
