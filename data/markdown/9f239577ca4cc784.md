#### Event Description

On 1-2 October, 2021, a team of Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC) visited NUCES-FAST, Peshawar Campus for re-accreditation of Batch 2017 & 2018 of BS Electrical Engineering. The team of PEC was comprising of Engr. Prof. Dr. Shahzad Amin Sheikh - CE & ME Rawalpindi, Engr. Dr. Irfan Khattak - UET Peshawar (Kohat Campus) and Engr. Jahan Zeb Khan - PEC HQ Islamabad. The team was accompanied and facilitated on the behalf of the Department by the OBE Coordinator & Assistant Professor, Dr. Asif Iqbal and OBE Data Analyst & Assistant Professor Mr. Maqsood M. Khan. The team thoroughly evaluated the program and had interaction with students of respective batches, faculty members, administrative staff, alumni and employers of our graduates. The visit was successfully concluded with an exit meeting with the Campus Director, Dr. Omar Usman Khan, and Head of Department, Dr. Muhammad Tariq.
