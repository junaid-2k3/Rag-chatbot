Dr. Muhammad Usman Abbasi is currently an Assistant Professor in the Electrical Engineering Department, National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (NUCES), Pakistan. He has done most of his PhD research at the Institute of Biomedical Engineering, the University of Oxford, United Kingdom. His current research interests include Brain-computer interfacing, biomedical signal processing, the Internet of Things, and Embedded Systems.
Dr. Usman is the author of several International Journals, conference papers and a book chapter. He holds several international certifications in the fields of Internet of Things, Microcontroller Interfacing and Embedded Systems.

### Education
