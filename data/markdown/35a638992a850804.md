#### Event Description

A self-constituted Review Panel visited National University of Computer & Emerging Sciences, Peshawar Campus for evaluation of MS and Ph.D. programs as per HEC guidelines on 2nd May 2019. During the visit, program files, student files, and faculty files were reviewed by the panel. Moreover, the panel had interaction with faculty members and students of both programs. At the end of the visit the panel submitted reports for both programs comprising of their observation and suggestion. Eminent members review panel were;

i.

Dr. Usman Ghani, Joint Director, Institute of Management Sciences, Hayatabad, Peshawar.

ii.

Dr. Ibrar Ali Shah, Chairman Department of Computer Software Engineering, University of Engineering & Technology, Mardan.
