#### Event Description

An Assessment Team visited Peshawar Campus for evaluation of BS Computer Science on Thursday 21-January-2021. Dr. Hasan Ali Khattak, Associate Professor, School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, National University of Science and Technology (NUST), Islamabad, and Mr. Fazl-e-Basit, Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science, NUCES, Peshawar were the invited subject experts for evaluation of the program. During the visit, the team evaluated the program while reviewing the Self-assessment Report, interacting with faculty members, interviewed students, and visited departmental facilities. The visit concluded with the sharing of AT findings and report to the Campus Director.
