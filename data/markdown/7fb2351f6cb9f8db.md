#### Event Description

A self-constituted Review Panel Visit to Peshawar Campus for Institutional Performance Evaluation (IPE) 2021-22 is successfully completed after three-day comprehensive assessment exercise from 25th till 27th May 2022. During the visit, the panel reviewed and evaluated University Portfolio Report (UPR) and University-wide Data (UwD) along with supporting documents. The panel interacted with faculty members, students, head of departments and representatives of campus management. Campus site visit for the evaluation of the provision of facilities was also performed.
Mr. Imran Ullah Khan Marwat, Director Quality Assurance, Higher Education Department, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Peshawar and Prof. Dr. Syed Waqar Shah, Chairman, Department of Electrical Engineering, University of Engineering and Technology, Peshawar were the eminent penal members
At the end of the third day the panel had an Exit Meeting with the Campus Director, where observations and findings were shared with them for improvement.
