Introduction
The National University of Computer & Emerging Sciences is a multi-campus private sector university set up 
under the Federal Charter granted by Ordinance No.XXIII of 2000, dated July 01, 2000, and is operating 
campuses at Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Peshawar, Faisalabad, and Multan.
Interested parties duly registered on the Active Taxpayer List, as well as registered charity organizations, non-
profits, and non-governmental organizations (NGOs) holding valid tax-exemption status are invited to provide 
Request for Bids (RFB) / Expression of Interest (EOI) to purchase the used computer items mentioned on 
Annex B
1. Instruction to Vendor/Bidder
1. Bidder must comply with conditions of Bidder Qualification Criteria defined in Annex-A.
2. In case of Joint Venture/subcontracting/subletting by the vendor, the university will only deal with 
responsible person/entity who submitted RFB/EOI.
3. Items are being auctioned on “as-is where-is” basis.

3. Bid Submission
1. Process will be through Open Competitive Bidding.
2. The bid shall be based on double envelope system. Outer envelope should contain:
1. Bidder Qualification Criteria (Annex-A)
2. Copy of valid NTN certificate
3. A signed and sealed sub-envelope containing the bid (See Annex-B)
3. All participating bidders must submit their bids by hand/by post on 31 July 2026. Bids can be 
submitted in person or submitted by post before the deadline.
4. Bid Opening
1. Bids will be opened on Monday, 3 August 2026 at 10:30 AM. 
2. Bids will be opened publicly in presence of bidders or their representatives who voluntarily choose 
to attend bid opening.
3. Financial proposals will be declared null and void if a sealed envelope is found to be tampered with 
by the disposal committee.
4. Bidder comparative statement will be prepared on same day of bid opening.

## Page 3

5. Bid Evaluation
1. Bids will be evaluated on the basis of comparative statement, bidder qualification criteria, and the 
vendor past experience with FAST NUCES (Approved Vendor List). 
2. Vendors interested in full lot procurement will receive extra weightage.
3. Evaluation decision will be communicated within 3 working days of bid opening. 
4. University reserves right to allocate selected lots/items directly to registered charity organizations 
before evaluating commercial bids
6. Contacting University for Clarifications & Queries
1. Contact may be made only to Mr. Ghulam Ali Abid Senior Manager IT at National University of 
Computer and Emerging Sciences, 160 Industrial Estate, Jamrud Road, Peshawar, OR by phone to: 
+92(0) 91 111 128 128 (Ext: 121), OR by Email: ghulam.ali@nu.edu.pk
2. Bidder is not allowed to contact any other employee of the NUCES-FAST on matters related to the 
bid.
3. No contact will be made on any matter from the time of opening of bid, to the final decision of 
awarding committee. If the bidder wishes to bring new information to the notice of committee, it 
shall do so in writing.
4. Any effort by the bidder to influence NUCES-FAST in its decision on bid evaluation, bid 
comparison or any matter related to the bid award may result in rejection of bidder’s bid.
7. Fee & Security
1. Bid fee of Rs. 0/- payable in shape of PO/DD/CDR/Cash must be attached with bid application in 
favor of BCCI-FAST NUCES (PHR OPER). Bid fee is waivered.
2. Upon successfully winning a bid, 10% of bid amount should be deposited by the successful bidder 
with the university’s account section within 2 working days of bid results announcement, and 
remaining amount shall be deposited within 7 working days of bid results announcemen.
3. Prevalent Tax rates on Public Auctions will be deducted from successful bidders. Registered charity 
organizations bidding for donation would be exempted from deduction on proof of tax exemption. 
4. Auctioned lot will be allowed to successful bidder on full payment only.
5. Auctioned lot will need to be collected by successful bidder immediately on full payment. The 
university will not be held liable for any damages / lost auctioned items after full payment is 
received.
