#### Event Description

Quality Enhancement Cell, Peshawar Campus arranged a training seminar on the Outcome-Based Education System for the faculty members on October 3, 2022. Dr. Omar Usman Khan, Director of Peshawar Campus and qualified trainer on OBE by the National Computing Education Accreditation Council (NCEAC) was the resource person for the session. A comparison of the OBE system with the conventional education system was presented for better understanding. In addition, elements of the OBE framework were discussed in detail along with a short briefing on Bloom’s Taxonomy.
