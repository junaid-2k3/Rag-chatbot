#### Event Description

The NUCES QEC of Peshawar Campus collaborated with the HR department of NUCES Islamabad Campus for the capacity building of employees of Peshawar Campus. Under this collaboration three (03) staff and one (01) faculty members were sent to Islamabad campus to attend a 1-Day Training Workshop on MS ADVANCED EXCEL on 4th March 2020. Ms. Maria Ibrar, Deputy Manager QEC, Mr. Muhammad Haroon, Accounts Officer, Mr. Mujadid Shah, PS to Director and Mr. Maqsood Muhammad Khan, OBE Data Analyst and Assistant Professor EE attended the said workshop. The facilitator of the training workshop was Mr. Abdul Shakoor, Chief Financial Officer, Boss Group of Companies
