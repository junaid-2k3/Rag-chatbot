#### Event Description

A self-constituted Review Panel Visit for Institutional Performance Evaluation 2022-23 at Peshawar Campus successfully concluded on 21st March, 2023.
Members of Review Panel were (i) Prof. Dr. Bakhtiar Khan Kasi, Dean Faculty of Information & Communication Technology, Balochistan University of Information Technology, Engineering & Management Sciences (BUITEMS), (ii) Prof. Dr. Sharifullah Khan, Dean Faculty of Electrical, Computer IT & Design, Pak-Austria Fachhochschule: Institute of Applied Sciences & Technology, Haripur, and (iii) Dr. Nouman Azam, Associate Professor, Department of Computer Science, NUCES-FAST, Peshawar Campus.
During the visit, the panel reviewed and evaluated the University Portfolio Report (UPR) and University-wide Data (UwD) along with supporting documents. The panel interacted with faculty members, students, heads of departments and representatives of campus management. Campus site visit for the evaluation of the provision of facilities was also performed.
