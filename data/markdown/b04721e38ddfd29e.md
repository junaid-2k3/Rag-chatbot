#### Event Description

QEC-PWR arranged an awareness seminar for the students on feedback mechanisms at NUCES and its importance. Ms. Maria Ibrar, Manager Quality Enhancement Cell of the Peshawar Campus was the resource person for the awareness session. The students were informed about the procedure through which their feedback is processed and presented at the decision-making forums of the university. In addition, students were educated to provide constructive feedback by being focused, clear statements rather than vague and ambiguous statements. The session was well taken by the students and ended with a question & answer session.
