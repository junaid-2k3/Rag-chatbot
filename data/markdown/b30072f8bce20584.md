#### Mr. Asad Khan on successfully defend PhD

* Posted On:  Dec 03, 21

Congratulation Mr. Asad Khan on successfully defending his PhD thesis titled: “Detection of N6-methyleadenosine (m6A) Sites from Transcriptomic Sequences ” under the supervision of Dr. Hafeez ur Rehman on 3rd December 2021 at Peshawar Campus. Mr. Asad Khan was on a fully-funded prestigious FAST PhD fellowship and has published a paper in a journal of computational science with an impact factor of 3.97. We congratulate the scholar and his supervisor on this great achievement.
