#### Event Description

On 10-Sep-2021, the first phase of training sessions under the faculty/staff development program concluded with the last training session on Asset Management System. The training session was conducted by Mr. Abdul Rehman, Internal Auditor, NUCES-FAST, Head Office. It was followed by the closing ceremony where the Campus Director, Dr. Omar Usman Khan thanked both the colleagues from Head Office for sparing time for Peshawar Campus to deliver two awareness sessions and two training sessions. He thanks all the faculty and staff members for their active participation in all the sessions by throwing discussions on the forum. At the end, shields were given as a token of thanks from the Peshawar Campus to the colleagues from Head Office.
