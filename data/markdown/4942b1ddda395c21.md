#### Mr. Suleman Mir successfully defend PhD

* Posted On:  Dec 10, 21

Congratulation Mr. Suleman Mir on successfully defending his PhD thesis titled: "Spectrum Sharing Between Radar and Communication System with Constraint" under the supervision of Dr. Inam Bari at Peshawar Campus on 10th of Dec 2021 at 10:30 am. The examination committee thoroughly examined the thesis and cross questioned the candidate on different aspects of the research. The candidate has published two research articles including one journal paper of international repute with an impact factor of 3.745 and one conference paper.
