#### FAANA visited Peshawar Campus

* Posted On:  Dec 01, 21

Mr. Rafaeel Akbar Chaudhry, President FAST-NU Alumni Association, North America - FAANA and FAST-NU Alumni Canada along with senior alumni Mr. Yaser Amir Awan (Director Labs, Mindstorm Studios) visited Peshawar Campus on December 1st, 2021 to engage all FAST-NU alumni and students to foster a lifelong intellectual and emotional connection between the University and its graduates.
