Our Library is based on INSIGNIA. Students can get details about issued books, available books, and other operations of the library. Book records of all other campuses are also available through this portal and if required, inter-campus book loan can also be requested.

# HEC Digital Library

The university is a member of the HEC Digital Library which provides access to high quality, peer-reviewed journals, databases, articles and e-Books across a wide range of disciplines.

# Network Library Server

Students can access books, FYP CD’s, courses slides, and other academic material through a library server available on local network of the university. To access, follow the following steps:

1. You will need to physically be in a computing lab
2. Launch \\libraryserver from Windows
3. Enter username:password credentials as student:123
