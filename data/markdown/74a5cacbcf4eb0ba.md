#### Event Description

Quality Enhancement Cell at Peshawar Campus organized 2-day training and awareness session under the faculty and staff development program 2022 on August 19 – 20, 2022. Mr. Yasir Nazir, Manager (HR-Admin), Mr. Abdul Rehman, Internal Auditor, and Syed Hassan Naqi, Deputy Treasurer from NUCES, Head Office were invited to the event.
An orientation session on HR policy and a training session on Efficient & Effective Office Administration and Management was delivered by Mr. Yasir Nazir. Mr. Abdul Rehman conducted a comprehensive session on Procurement Policy and associated matters. However, Syed Hassan Naqi briefed all participants on the Employee’s Provident Fund. All the sessions were well taken and participated by the faculty and staff of the Peshawar Campus.
