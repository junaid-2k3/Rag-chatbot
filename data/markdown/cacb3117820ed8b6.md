2. Director Message

I am delighted to welcome you to our campus, located in Peshawars residential suburb of Hayatabad. We take pride in offering a conducive learning environment, with state-of-the-art infrastructure, highly qualified faculty, well equipped labs, and a student-centric approach. Our academic programs are all accredited with the Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC), and the National Computing Education & Accreditation Council (NCEAC), and are regularly updated based on input from, and our liaison with industry. As such, this makes our graduates highly sought after. I invite you to explore our campus, discover our facilities, and learn more about our programs. We are committed to providing a world-class education and fostering a community of innovators, thinkers, and leaders. Thank you for considering our university as your academic destination. I look forward to welcoming you to our campus community!

**Dr. Omar Usman Khan**
Professor; Department of Computer Science
