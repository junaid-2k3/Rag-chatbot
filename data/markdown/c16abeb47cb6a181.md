#### Event Description

Quality Enhancement Cell at Peshawar Campus organized a one-day Teacher Assistants Training Workshop on August 10, 2022. Ms. Sara Rehmat, Lecturer, Department of Computer Science facilitated the workshop. The workshop was organized to communicate the importance and the responsibilities associated with Teacher Assistantships for the students entering the professional world. The training workshop was graced by the Director Campus, Dr. Omar Usman Khan with his opening remarks.
