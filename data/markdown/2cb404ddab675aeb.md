#### Event Description

The NCEAC (National Computing Education Accreditation Council) paid an Interim Visit for BS Software Engineering program on October 31, 2022. Prof. Dr. Jamil Ahmad, Vice Chairman NCEAC, and Vice-Chancellor Hazara University Mansehra visited Peshawar Campus. A detailed presentation about the BS (SE) program was given by the HoD (Computer Science), Dr. Hafeez Ur Rehman, followed by the document review, interaction with faculty & students, and inspection of the facilities available for the program. The evaluator concluded the visit on a positive note by appreciating and commending the facilities available for students and also suggested a few areas for improvement in an exit meeting with the Campus Director and HoD (Computer Science).
