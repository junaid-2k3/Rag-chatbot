#### Event Description

A Training for Teaching Assistants of NUCES FAST Peshawar Campus was organized by QEC on Thursday 28th October 2021. Dr. Taimoor Khan, Assistant Professor was the resource person for the said training session. The objectives of the training session were to train TAs on the responsibilities associated with teaching assistantships that primarily include managerial skills and adaptation of professional mannerisms.
