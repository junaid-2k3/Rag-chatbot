Mr. Usama Musharaf is currently working as a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science at National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (FAST-NUCES), Peshawar Campus. He obtained his Msc (Computer Science) from University of Peshawar and MS (Computer Science) from CECOS University of IT & Emerging Sciences Peshawar in 2015 and 2020 respectively. His research areas are Software Engineering, Microservices Architecture, and DevOps.

### Education
