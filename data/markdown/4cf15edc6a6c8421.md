#### Event Description

Under the faculty/staff development program, Peshawar Campus planned a series of workshops for the Fall semester of 2021. Under this program, on 8-Sept-2021, two awareness sessions on HR Policies of NUCES-FAST and Procurement Policy of NUCES-FAST were conducted. Session on HR policy was conducted by Mr. Yasir Nazir, Manager HR-Admin, NUCES, FAST Head Office while the session on Procurement policy was conducted by Mr. Abdul Rehman, Internal Auditor, NUCES-FAST, Head Office. The sessions were well participated by the participants and comments & concerns were noted by the colleagues from Head Office for consideration & perusal of top management.
On 9-Sep-2021, a training session on Documentation, Record Management, & Minute Taking was delivered by Mr. Yasir Nazir, Manager HR-Admin, NUCES, FAST Head Office. The session was attended by the academic & non-academic departmental heads, program coordinators, and conveners of the committees like the Campus Purchase Committee, Graduate Studies Committee, etc.
QEC, Peshawar is the organizing department of the campus.
