#### Event Description

HR department in collaboration with QEC Peshawar organized a two-day training workshop on IT Essentials - MS Excel for our campus staff members on May 4th and 5th, 2023. Mr. Sohaib Shah conducted the session, and our participants found it informative and valuable. The workshop also included a short briefing by QEC on the Erasmus+ project, supporting the accreditation of programs and internationalization of South Asian HEIs. We were honored to have Dr. Omar Usman Khan, our Director Campus, attend and encourage our staff members to participate actively. Overall, it was a successful workshop, and we expect it to have a positive impact on our staff members' skills and productivity. Thank you to all who participated!
