## Campus Facilities

1. [Home](http://pwr.nu.edu.pk/)
2. Campus Facilities

## Student Cafeteria

The campus has a cafeteria for students and staff where all cultural and traditional foods are available according to student’s tastes, with a cyclic menu. The cafeteria is completely Air Conditioned and also possesses a table tennis table.

---

## WiFi Internet

The campus has a hi-speed WI-FI internet facility available for student’s internet access, LAN based ports are also available. This facility is accessible throughout the campus.

---

## Bus Facility

The University provides transport to pick and drop students from selected points on a self-finance basis. There are two buses for this purpose, which cover almost all the areas of Peshawar.

---

## e-Library

There is a campus e-library where students can access all course books as well as a reference book of their prospective courses. This is only accessible on campus premises.

---

## Computer Labs

The campus provides computer facilities along with internet access in computer labs where students can utilize computers for their academic, as well as personal purposes.

---

## Sports Area

The campus has a sports area where students can do extra-curricular activities. The sports area includes Basket Ball Court, Volleyball field, Badminton Court and Table Tennis Table(s) as well as a futsal ground.

---

## Projector/LCDS

Campus classrooms are completely equipped with projectors. Lectures are delivered on presentation slides, these projectors are also used for extra co-curricular activities too.

---

## FBO Movie

FAST Box Office organizes to play different interactive Hollywood movies as well as academic documentaries frequently on the basis of a prescribed schedule after university time.

---

## SLATE Access

University is using slate as an online learning management system, where students have access to lectures, assignments, announcements, and grades.

---

## Social Societies

There are different social societies on campus for student’s grooming, learning and enhancing organizational and other skills. They are also for enjoyment and fun.

---

## Girl’s Common Room

The campus has a Girl’s Common Room where all the female students of campus can sit and spend spare time studying and doing fun activities. The room is equipped with Gym equipment and board games items.

---

## Parking Area

University has a secure parking facility in the campus; for staff, faculty and students of the campus.
