1. [Home](http://pwr.nu.edu.pk/)
2. Campus GEIAH Committee

The National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (NUCES), Peshawar Campus is committed to fostering an all-inclusive and supportive learning and working environment. In compliance with the Higher Education Commission’s (HEC) Policy on Protection against Sexual Harassment in Higher Education Institutions, NUCES has a zero-tolerance policy for any untoward conduct that violates the dignity of any individual within the campus. As such, all matters of harassment, fooling, intimidation, discrimination on gender / ethnic grounds, and social pressure are addressed through this policy.

## Reporting Process

All complaints need to be reported to Director campus directly. In case of students, cases may be referred either to Director campus or to any member of the GEIAH Committee. In case of confidentiality, complainant may report the complaint through a confidant colleague who is willing to carry on reporting as well as case processing through concerned officials on behalf of the anonymized complainant.

## Case Processing

Director campus will refer all cases to GEIAH committee who will carry out processing in accordance with set criteria. For in-process cases, documentation record is in custody of convener GEIAH committee. For concluded cases, documentation is kept on file after anonymization process. Statistics of cases may be obtained from Secretary GEIAH committee. Any information sharing involving names/case details would be processed with explicit written permission of Director campus.
