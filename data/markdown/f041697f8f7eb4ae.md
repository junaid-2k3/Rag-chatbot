#### Event Description

A self-constituted Review Panel visited NUCES(FAST), Peshawar Campus for Institutional Performance Evaluation (IPE). It was 3-day visit from 2nd till 4th April 2019 where University Portfolio Report and University-wide data along with pertinent evidence were reviewed and evaluated. The team had interactions with the Director Campus, HoDs, Sectional Heads, faculty members and students. The team also visited available facilities for quality education. After exit meeting, where the team shared their findings with the Director, a comprehensive report is submitted for enhancement and further improvement of quality education provided at Peshawar Campus. Eminent members review panel were;

i.

Prof. Dr. Muhammad Abid, Dean, Faculty of Numerical and Physical Sciences, University of Peshawar, Peshawar.

ii.

Prof. Dr. Mohammad Inayatullah Babar, Department of Electrical Engineering, University of Engineering & Technology, Peshawar.
