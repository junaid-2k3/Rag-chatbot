#### Vehicle, Generator, AC Auction

**Closing Date:**  24-May-2023

Individuals or organizations interested in acquiring old Vehicles, Generators, and AC Units completing their use at campus may submit their sealed proposals, labeled as "confidential" via postal mail to the Convener Disposal Committee. Successful bidders who meet the reserve price will receive notification by 1st June 2023. They are required to submit 10% earnest money to the campus accounts section within one week, and the remaining amount within three weeks (including the earnest period).
