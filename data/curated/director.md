## Director of FAST-NUCES Peshawar campus

The Director of the FAST-NUCES Peshawar campus (also called the Campus Director or Director Campus, the head of the campus) is Dr. Omar Usman Khan. He is a Professor in the Department of Computer Science and also writes the Director's Message on the campus website.

This is stated on his faculty profile ("Dr. Omar Usman Khan is the Director Campus and Professor at FAST-NUCES Peshawar") and in campus news reports that refer to him as Campus Director.
